Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

MyWay Travel App ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Use

We prioritize your privacy and do not collect any personal information that can identify you directly (such as your name, email address, or phone number) without your explicit consent.

  • Usage Data: We collect anonymous technical information to improve app performance and user experience. This includes device type, operating system version, and general app usage patterns.
  • Unique Identifiers: We may use anonymous device identifiers to track aggregate analytics.

2. Location Data

To provide core features like navigation and "nearby" attraction suggestions, the app requires access to your device's location services.

  • Processing: Location data is primarily processed locally on your device.
  • Storage: We do not store your real-time location or travel history on our permanent servers.

3. Third-Party Services

We use reputable third-party services to enhance our services. These providers have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase: Used for analytics, remote configuration, and crash reporting. Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat: Used to manage in-app subscriptions and purchase validation. RevenueCat Privacy Policy

4. Cookies, Web Beacons & Third-Party Advertising

Like any other website, MyWay uses "cookies" on our web platform. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookie:

Google is one of the third-party vendors on our website. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to www.mywaytravelapp.com and other sites on the internet. However, visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on MyWay, which are sent directly to users' browser. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see on websites that you visit.

Note that MyWay has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

5. Third-Party Privacy Policies & Opt-Out

MyWay's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information. It may include their practices and instructions about how to opt-out of certain options.

You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. To know more detailed information about cookie management with specific web browsers, it can be found at the browsers' respective websites. You can also opt out of personalized advertising by visiting AboutAds.info Choices or Google Ads Settings.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
