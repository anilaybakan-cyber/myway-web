App support, guide corrections, partnerships and press.

Get in touch

Questions about the app, corrections to a guide, partnership or press enquiries: we read every message.

support@mywaytravelapp.com

We usually reply within 1–3 business days.

What to include

  • Guide corrections: the city, the place or section, and what has changed (for example a closure or new opening hours).
  • App support: your device model, operating system version and app version (found in the app settings).
  • Account and data requests: the email address associated with your account. See our privacy policy for details on how we handle personal data.
