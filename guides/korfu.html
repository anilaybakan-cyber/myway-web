Overview & Atmosphere

The True Vibe of Corfu

The grand dame of the Ionian Sea, Corfu (Kerkyra) is a sun-kissed fusion of Venetian nobility, French elegance, and British flair. Its UNESCO-listed Old Town is a maze of cobblestone alleys ('kantounia') winding between pastel Renaissance mansions where laundry flutters overhead, guarded by two colossal Venetian sea fortresses. With the Parisian-style Liston arcades, the emerald cliffs and sea caves of Paleokastritsa, Empress Sisi's mythological Achilleion Palace, and tangy kumquat liqueur, Corfu is pure Mediterranean aristocracy.

🗣️ Local Concept: Kantounia (kahn-too-nyah)

The narrow, winding, cobblestone Venetian alleyways of Corfu Old Town, barely wide enough for two people, shaded by multi-story pastel mansions.

3 Unwritten Rules for Corfu

1

Get lost in the Campiello quarter without a map

Wander through the oldest Venetian residential district of the Old Town, discovering tiny cobblestone squares, hidden chapels, and wrought-iron balconies.

2

Arrive at Paleokastritsa Monastery in the morning

Perched on a dramatic clifftop above twin turquoise bays, visit the 13th-century monastery early before taking a small boat into the sea caves.

3

Sip an iced frappe beneath the Liston arcades

Modeled after Paris's Rue de Rivoli by French architects, sit under the lanterned arches facing the green Spianada cricket pitch for classic people-watching.

Getting Around

Corfu Public Transit & Airport Transfers

Blue city buses serve the suburbs and Achilleion, while Green buses connect island-wide beaches.

🚶 Walking the Old Town

The Liston, Old Fortress, New Fortress, and Campiello alleys are all pedestrian-only.

🚌 Blue City Buses

Line 15 connects the airport to town center; Line 10 runs directly to Achilleion Palace; Line 2A goes to Kanoni.

🚌 Green Regional Buses (KTEL)

Depart from the central bus station to Paleokastritsa, Sidari (Canal d'Amour), and Kavos.

🚗 Rental Car or Scooter

Ideal for exploring hidden western clifftops, Cape Drastis, and Porto Timoni twin beaches.

⛵ Boat Taxis & Cave Tours

Small wooden boats at Paleokastritsa bay take visitors into the Blue Eye and Nausicaa sea caves.

💡 Kanoni Airport View

Sit at the cafe terrace on Kanoni hill to watch jetliners land right over the blue lagoon waters just meters above the sea.

Seasons & Weather

Best Time to Visit Corfu

The world-famous 'Botides' pot-smashing Easter, warm summer coves, and golden autumn harvests.

Spring Apr – May
Corfu Easter & Pot-Smashing (Botides)

On Holy Saturday at 11:00 AM, thousands of massive red clay jugs ('Botides') filled with water are hurled from top-floor balconies crashing onto the streets below.

Summer Jun – Aug
Emerald Coves & Seaside Tavernas

Sun-drenched days swimming in Paleokastritsa's emerald coves, boat trips to Canal d'Amour, and sunset cocktails on clifftops.

Autumn Sep – Oct
Kumquat Harvest & Gentle Warmth

The summer crowds depart, sea waters remain delightfully warm, and distilleries harvest bright orange kumquat fruit.

Winter Nov – Mar
Lush Greenery & Cultural Calm

Corfu receives winter rains that keep its 4 million olive trees famously green, while philharmonic concerts echo in theaters.

Culinary Culture

Authentic Local Dining & Food Secrets in Corfu

Garlicky beef Sofrito, spicy tomato Pastitsada, and aromatic Kumquat liqueur.

🥩 Corfiot Beef Sofrito

Tender veal cutlets slow-braised in dry white wine, garlic, vinegar, and flat-leaf parsley, served over buttery mashed potatoes or rice.

⭐ Venetian Heritage
🍝 Corfiot Pastitsada

Braised beef or rooster simmered in rich tomato gravy heavily spiced with 'spetsieriko' (cinnamon, cloves, nutmeg, allspice), served over thick tubular macaroni.

⭐ Sunday Classic
🐟 Bourdeto Fish Stew

Fresh scorpionfish or cod simmered in a fiery, concentrated red pepper and paprika broth with onions and lemon juice. A fisherman's staple.

⭐ Spicy Seafood
🍊 Kumquat Liqueur & Candies

Bright orange mini-citrus fruit brought from China in 1860; distilled into sweet, aromatic liqueurs, marmalades, and candied sweets.

⭐ Island Symbol
🥤 Tsitsibira (Ginger Beer)

Traditional non-alcoholic fermented ginger beer brewed with fresh ginger root, lemon juice, and raisins; a beloved legacy of British rule since 1860.

⭐ British Heritage
🐟 Bianco Fish

Fresh whitefish gently poached in a delicate sauce of extra-virgin olive oil, garlic, lemon juice, white pepper, and potatoes.

⭐ Delicate Classic
🍷 The Venetian Well

Dine at The Venetian Well on secluded Kremasti Square—one of Greece's most romantic courtyards, centered on a 1699 sculpted stone well.

Off The Beaten Path

Handpicked Hidden Gems in Corfu

Twin sea coves, Byzantine eagles' nests, and sculpted sandstone fjords.

Vlacherna Monastery & Mouse Island Postcard Icon

A 17th-century whitewashed nunnery connected to Kanoni by a narrow pedestrian sea causeway, framed by green cypress islets.

Porto Timoni Twin Beach Double Beach

A dramatic isthmus separating two simultaneous turquoise bays near Afionas, reached via a rewarding 20-minute scenic cliff hike.

Achilleion Palace of Empress Sisi Royal Palace

Neoclassical summer palace built in 1890 for Empress Elisabeth of Austria, adorned with dramatic marble statues of Dying Achilles.

Angelokastro (Castle of Angels) Byzantine Fortress

A sheer 300-meter clifftop Byzantine fortress perched precariously over the Ionian Sea, offering panoramic views of the entire island.

Cape Drastis Sandstone Cliffs Scenic Cliffs

Spectacular weathered white clay cliffs and dramatic finger-like sea stacks jutting into turquoise waters on the northwest tip.

Gouvia Venetian Naval Shipyard Historic Relic

Three soaring 18th-century stone archways surviving from the colossal Venetian naval drydock and arsenal on Gouvia Bay.

✨ Angelokastro Sunset

Visit Angelokastro in the late afternoon; the view of the sun melting into the horizon from 300 meters above the sea is unforgettable.

Scam Prevention

Tourist Traps to Avoid & Smarter Alternatives

Insider tips to experience the authentic aristocratic character of Corfu.

☕ TRAP

Overpaying for generic coffee on the front Liston strip

Front-row cafe seats on the Liston charge a premium; step into the narrow Campiello alleys for family-run cafes with twice the charm at half the price.

💡 What to Do Instead: Find a cozy courtyard table behind Saint Spyridon Church.
👟 TRAP

Wearing flip-flops to hike Porto Timoni

The path to Porto Timoni is steep, rocky, and slippery; attempting the descent in beach slides often leads to sprained ankles.

💡 What to Do Instead: Wear sneakers or hiking sandals and carry plenty of water.
🚗 TRAP

Driving a large car into Old Town alleys

Corfu's Venetian streets are narrow, with historic arched stone entries; driving into the Old Town is prohibited and tickets are strict.

💡 What to Do Instead: Park at the Old Port or Spianada parking areas.
🚤 TRAP

Missing the sea cave boat ride at Paleokastritsa

The most vivid emerald-blue waters are hidden inside sea caves only accessible by small local boats.

💡 What to Do Instead: Spend €12 for a 30-minute wooden boat trip into the Nausicaa cave.
🛡️ Church Dress Code

When visiting Saint Spyridon Church or Paleokastritsa Monastery, ensure shoulders and knees are modestly covered.
