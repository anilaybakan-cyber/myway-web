Mykonos

One of the world's most expensive islands — but this is also where you find the spirit of the Aegean islands in its purest form

✍️ MyWay Editorial Team • 🛡️ Verified Local Guide
Overview & Atmosphere

The True Vibe of Mykonos

Mykonos is like two different islands. The first: some of the world's most expensive beach clubs, mega yachts, nightlife and Paradise Beach packed to bursting in July and August. The second: whitewashed walls, narrow labyrinthine streets, windmills and blue-domed Chora, hidden at the heart of the island. This second Mykonos shows itself in the early morning hours or out of season. The island is small — everything is reachable by vehicle or bus. But before coming to Mykonos, one thing needs to be accepted: everything here is expensive and in midsummer it is very crowded. If you come at peace with that reality, Mykonos won't disappoint.

🗣️ Local Concept: Chora (HO-ra)

Greek for 'town' or 'city' — the main town of every island in the Aegean is called Chora. In Mykonos, Chora is the white labyrinthine heart of the island. Narrow streets, whitewashed walls, blue doors, climbing plants. Deliberately designed like a maze — in the old days to confuse pirates. Today getting lost is just as easy and just as beautiful.

3 Unwritten Rules for Mykonos

1

If you want to come in July-August, prepare your budget and your patience

In midsummer Mykonos fills with crowds from all over the world. Hotel prices are very high, restaurant reservations are essential, finding a spot on the beaches is difficult. June or September offers the same sunshine and sea, prices noticeably lower and crowds at a tolerable level.

2

Explore Chora in the early morning hours

During the day Chora's narrow streets get clogged with tourist crowds. But between 7am and 9am the streets are nearly empty — apart from cats and pigeons. At this hour it's possible to take photos, have a quiet coffee and get lost. Beautiful in the evenings too, but the early morning is something else entirely.

3

Hire a vehicle — ideal for discovering the island in a day

Mykonos is small but KTEL buses don't go everywhere. Hiring an ATV is the most practical way to reach the island's northern coves, Ano Mera village and hidden beaches. Small car or motorbike are also options. In midsummer, hire in the early morning — by afternoon the good vehicles are gone.

Getting Around

Mykonos Public Transit & Airport Transfers

Mykonos is a small island. KTEL buses cover the main spots; an ATV or vehicle is essential for the rest.

Flight from Athens approximately 45 minutes, ticket €40-120 depending on season and how far in advance. Ferry: fast ferry from Piraeus (Seajet, Golden Star) 2-2.5 hours, €50-80. From Rafina port 1.5-2 hours, slightly cheaper. Rafina is close to Athens Airport — KTEL bus 30-40 minutes, €4. Buy tickets in advance in summer — sells out.

Mykonos has two bus terminals: Fabrika (south) and Old Port (north) — no connection between them but 15 minutes on foot through Chora. From Fabrika: Paradise Beach (~15 min) and the airport (~10 min). From Old Port: the new port and Agios Stefanos. Ticket €1-3, cash from the driver.

The most free-spirited way to explore the island. ATV daily €25-50, small car €40-70, motorbike €20-40. Essential for reaching the northern coves, Ano Mera village, Fokos and Agios Sostis beaches. Driving licence required. Hire in the early morning — by afternoon the best vehicles are gone. Return with a full tank.

Daily boat trips to Delos Island depart from Old Port pier — return approximately €20-25. Combined ticket with Rhenia €25-30. Private boat and catamaran hire also available. Water taxi between beaches: Platis Gialos to Paradise Beach by water taxi 10-15 minutes, €10-15.

Chora's labyrinth is walkable — small and compact. From the windmills to Little Venice 10 minutes, Paraportiani 5 minutes. Getting out of Chora requires KTEL or a vehicle. Coastal walking path and Chora-Ano Mera trail for the more adventurous.

⚡ Pro Tip

Daily boat trips from Mykonos Old Port. Centre of the ancient Greek pantheon — Temple of Apollo, lion statues, mosaic-floored houses. UNESCO World Heritage Site. Archaeological museum on the island. Return boat ticket plus archaeological site entry combined total €30-35. Take the first morning sailing. Very hot in summer — hat and water essential.

Off The Beaten Path

Handpicked Hidden Gems in Mykonos

Mykonos's finest corners are far from the beach clubs — on the north coast, in quiet coves and in the early morning hours.

Agios Sostis beach

A small cove on the northern part of the island, reached by vehicle or a long walk. No beach club, no sunbeds, no bar — just sand, rock and clean water. Kiki's Taverna alongside, no reservations and with a queue but both delicious and affordable. Even in midsummer, far from the crowds.

Delos Island

30 minutes by boat from Mykonos Old Port. The sacred island of ancient Greece — where Apollo and Artemis are said to have been born. The Terrace of the Lions, mosaic-floored houses, temple ruins. UNESCO World Heritage Site. Take the first morning sailing, bring a hat and water. Closed for visits in winter.

Paraportiani Church

At the northern end of Chora, on a slope facing the sea. A cumulative white structure made up of five separate chapels. Considered one of the world's most photographed churches. Nearly empty between 7am and 8am. In the afternoon, crowds with cameras at every corner. The church interior is small and plain but the atmosphere is very powerful.

Fokos beach and Fokos Taverna

In the north-east of the island, at the end of a steep road reached by vehicle. Completely natural, no facilities. The adjacent Fokos Taverna is known as Mykonos's finest fish restaurant — local produce, fresh fish, no reservations. Quiet even in midsummer. ATV or car essential. Plan a day trip that includes lunch here.

Cine Manto

In the centre of Chora, inside a garden. A tradition much loved in Greece: open-air cinema on summer nights, continuing since the 1960s. Old Hollywood classics and modern films under the pine trees. Watching a film at night with tsipouro (local distilled spirit) and meze is another experience altogether. Ticket €10-12, reservation recommended.

Ano Mera village

In the centre of the island, the island's only real inland village. The square around Panagia Tourliani Monastery, local cafés and tavernas. Very different from Mykonos Chora's white labyrinth — simpler, more local, quieter. A small market every day of the week. 15-20 minutes from Chora by car.

(YAH-sas)

(pah-rah-kah-LOH)

(YAH-mas)
