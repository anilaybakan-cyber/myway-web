Leipzig

City of music, books, and peaceful revolution: Bach's St. Thomas, historic arcades, and industrial canals

✍️ MyWay Editorial Team • 🛡️ Verified Local Guide
Overview & Atmosphere

The True Vibe of Leipzig

Saxony's vibrant cultural powerhouse, Leipzig is steeped in musical and literary legend: Johann Sebastian Bach directed the St. Thomas Boys Choir here for 27 years, Felix Mendelssohn founded Germany's first conservatory, and Johann Wolfgang von Goethe set pivotal scenes of Faust in its vaulted wine cellars. In 1989, prayers at St. Nicholas Church sparked the Peaceful Revolution that brought down the Berlin Wall. Today, dubbed 'Hypezig', it pulsates with youthful creative energy, historic trading arcades, converted cotton-mill art factories in Plagwitz, and serene tree-shaded canals.

🗣️ Local Concept: Gose (go-zeh)

Leipzig's centuries-old heritage sour wheat beer, brewed with coriander and sea salt instead of excessive hops, giving it a deliciously refreshing, tart, and crisp character.

3 Unwritten Rules for Leipzig

1

Visit Bach's tomb in St. Thomas Church (Thomaskirche)

Step into the choir of the Gothic church where J.S. Bach worked as cantor from 1723 until his death in 1750, and hear the world-famous Thomanerchor sing on Friday evenings or Sundays.

2

Trace Faust's footsteps in Mädler-Passage and Auerbachs Keller

Descend into the 1525 vaulted wine cellar beneath Mädler-Passage where Goethe set the legendary scene where Mephistopheles rides a wine barrel out into the street.

3

Kayak the canals of Plagwitz and explore the Spinnerei

Rent a canoe on the Karl-Heine-Kanal and explore the Spinnerei, a colossal former cotton mill transformed into Europe's leading modern art gallery complex.

Getting Around

Leipzig Public Transit & Airport Transfers

LVB's extensive tram and S-Bahn network makes exploring both historic passages and canal quarters effortless.

🚆

Airport S-Bahn (S5 & S5X)

Direct S-Bahn trains whisk passengers between Leipzig/Halle Airport and Leipzig Hbf in just 14 minutes.

🚋

Tram Network (LVB)

13 tram lines fan out from the central station ring to Plagwitz, Südvorstadt, and the Monument to the Battle of the Nations.

🚶

Walking the Old Town Ring

The entire historic center within the 'Promenadenring' is pedestrianized and walkable in 15 minutes.

🛶

Canoes & Kayaks on Canals

Leipzig has over 200 km of navigable waterways; rent a canoe at Bootsverleih Klingerweg to paddle through Plagwitz.

🎫

LEIPZIG CARD

Unlimited travel on all public transport across the city plus up to 50% discount on museums, city tours, and boat trips.

💡 Leipzig Hbf Shopping

Leipzig Central Station houses a multi-level shopping promenade with over 140 shops open late, even on Sundays.

Neighborhood Guide

Where to Stay in Leipzig: Best Areas

From the musical heritage of the Old Town to the industrial artist lofts of Plagwitz and the nightlife of Südvorstadt.

Zentrum & Marktplatz

Heart & Music

Steps from Thomaskirche, Old Town Hall (Altes Rathaus), Mädler-Passage, and the Gewandhaus concert hall.

📍 Central • Music • Historic

Plagwitz & Schleußig

Art Lofts & Canals

Industrial heritage quarter; former textile factories turned into art lofts, indie cafes on Karl-Heine-Straße, and canal boat docks.

📍 Art Lofts • Canals • Hip

Südvorstadt (KarLi)

Nightlife & Bohemian

Centered along Karl-Liebknecht-Straße ('KarLi'); vibrant pub terraces, indie cinemas, craft burger joints, and street art.

📍 Nightlife • Student • Bars

Musikviertel

Elegant & Culture

Stately 19th-century bourgeois mansions, the Federal Administrative Court, university library, and lush Clara-Zetkin Park.

📍 Grand • Quiet • Parks

Gohlis

Baroque & Peaceful

North of the center; tranquil residential area with the rococo Gohliser Schlösschen palace and Schiller's historic summer cottage.

📍 Baroque • Green • Peaceful
Seasons & Weather

Best Time to Visit Leipzig

The world-renowned Bachfest in June, vibrant canal paddling in summer, and cozy winter book fairs.

Spring

Mar – May
Leipzig Book Fair (Buchmesse)

March turns the city into a celebration of reading with the Leipzig Reads (Leipzig liest) festival hosting 3,000 public readings in cafes, churches, and galleries.

Summer

Jun – Aug
Bachfest & Canal Season

In June, musicians from around the globe perform Bach's cantatas and passions in historic churches, while canals fill with kayaks.

Autumn

Sep – Nov
Festival of Lights (Lichtfest 9. Oktober)

Tens of thousands gather on Augustusplatz with candles on October 9 to remember the historic Monday demonstrations that toppled the wall.

Winter

Nov – Dec
Leipziger Weihnachtsmarkt

Over 300 illuminated wooden stalls fill Marktplatz and Naschmarkt with medieval Finnish villages, artisan glass, and spiced hot Gose.

Culinary Culture

Authentic Local Dining & Food Secrets in Leipzig

Sweet marzipan Leipziger Lerche, sour coriander-salted Gose beer, and rich Sächsischer Sauerbraten.

🥧

Leipziger Lerche

Flaky shortcrust pastry cup filled with rich almond-marzipan paste and a dollop of strawberry jam, topped with crossed pastry ribbons.

⭐ Pastry Heritage
🥗

Leipziger Allerlei

Classic gourmet vegetable dish made with baby carrots, asparagus, morel mushrooms, and sweet peas, traditionally garnished with freshwater crayfish tails and semolina dumplings.

⭐ Culinary Classic
🍺

Leipziger Gose

Historic top-fermented sour wheat ale brewed with coriander and salt, served in long cylindrical glasses pure or mixed with fruit liqueur (Allasch or raspberry).

⭐ Historic Ale
🥩

Sächsischer Sauerbraten

Tender beef marinated in vinegar, red wine, and spices, slow-braised with sweet gingerbread (Lebkuchen) sauce, served with red cabbage and potato dumplings.

⭐ Saxon Roast
🥞

Quarkkeulchen

Pan-fried golden Saxon patties made from mashed potatoes and quark curd cheese, dusted with cinnamon sugar and served with cold applesauce.

⭐ Sweet Treat
☕

Coffe Baum Heritage Coffee

A steaming cup of Melange or filter coffee enjoyed in Europe's oldest surviving coffee temple, where Goethe, Bach, and Schumann were regulars.

⭐ Coffee Culture
🍷 Bayerischer Bahnhof Gose

Visit Bayerischer Bahnhof, Germany's oldest preserved railway station (1842), now an award-winning microbrewery producing fresh Gose on tap.

Off The Beaten Path

Handpicked Hidden Gems in Leipzig

Cotton-mill art factories, monumental battle memorials, and secret canal passages.

✨ Art Powerhouse

Baumwollspinnerei Art Factory

Once Europe's largest cotton spinning mill; now home to over 100 artist studios, contemporary galleries (including Neo Rauch), and art cinemas.

✨ Monumental

Völkerschlachtdenkmal (Battle of the Nations)

Europe's tallest war monument (91 meters), commemorating Napoleon's 1813 defeat. Climb 500 steps for awe-inspiring views and whispering gallery acoustics.

✨ Historic Arcades

Mädler-Passage & Barthels Hof Courtyards

Stroll through magnificent multi-story glass-roofed 19th-century shopping arcades and baroque merchant courtyards with carved sandstone balconies.

✨ Canal Icon

Karl-Heine-Kanal & Stelzenhaus

Paddle beneath weeping willows past the Stelzenhaus, a historic industrial corrugated-steel warehouse perched dramatically on concrete stilts above the water.

✨ Grand Library

Bibliotheca Albertina Reading Hall

A majestic neo-Renaissance university library featuring soaring marble staircases, classical statues, and an ornate glass-domed reading hall.

✨ Rococo Pavilion

Gohliser Schlösschen

A late-rococo country pleasure palace in the north of the city with frescoed ballrooms, manicured parterre gardens, and classical piano recitals.

✨ Monument Crypt Acoustics

Stand in the colossal crypt of the Völkerschlachtdenkmal; the acoustics carry the softest hum for more than 10 seconds.

Scam Prevention

Tourist Traps to Avoid & Smarter Alternatives

Essential insider advice to navigate Leipzig's culture and history with ease.

🍺 TRAP

Expecting Gose to taste like standard pilsner

Gose is sour, salty, and spiced with coriander; first-time visitors expecting sweet lager are often surprised. Embrace the refreshing tartness!

💡 What to Do Instead: Try Gose 'pur' first, or order it with a shot of caraway liqueur ('Gose mit Allasch').
🚆 TRAP

Rushing through Leipzig Central Station

Leipzig Hbf is an architectural monument spanning 26 platforms with a three-story shopping gallery; don't just dash to your train.

💡 What to Do Instead: Take 20 minutes to admire the stone grand hall and historic steam locomotives on track 24.
⛪ TRAP

Only viewing Nikolaikirche from the outside

The unassuming exterior of St. Nicholas Church conceals a breathtaking pastel neoclassical interior with palm-tree-shaped columns.

💡 What to Do Instead: Step inside to see the pastel green-and-pink palm pillars where the 1989 revolution began.
🛶 TRAP

Only taking public trams through Plagwitz

The true industrial and artistic magic of Plagwitz is hidden along the canals, accessible best by kayak or waterside footpaths.

💡 What to Do Instead: Rent a canoe or walk the Karl-Heine-Kanal towpath on foot.
🛡️ Coffee Culture Pride

Saxons love coffee so passionately they were historically called 'Kaffeesachsen'—sitting for afternoon coffee and cake is a sacred daily ritual.

Local Slang

Useful Everyday Phrases in Leipzig

Charming expressions from the warm Saxon dialect.

🗣️ Gudn Tach!
Friendly Saxon greeting meaning 'Good day!'
🗣️ Helle!
Saxon expression for someone who is sharp, clever, and quick-witted.
🗣️ Eine Gose, bitte
'One Gose sour wheat beer, please'—order this in local taverns.
🗣️ Dankschön
Sincere Saxon 'Thank you very much'.
🗣️ Tschüssi!
Playful, affectionate everyday 'Goodbye!'
