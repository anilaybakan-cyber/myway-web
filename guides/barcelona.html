Barcelona

Gaudí, sun, sea — and always, always Catalan first

Overview

The Vibe of Barcelona

Walking through Barcelona, you might find yourself inside a fairy tale, a medieval castle, or a massive beach party — sometimes all three in the same afternoon. This is a proud city that identifies itself not as Spanish, but Catalan first. There are no straight lines here. Gaudí saw to that. Time moves differently. Dinner starts at 9 PM. The party begins after midnight.

🗣️ Local Concept: Bon dia (bon DEE-ah)

Good morning in Catalan — not Spanish. Say this when you walk into any shop and watch the doors open. This one small word signals that you see Barcelona as it sees itself.

Insider Advice

3 Unwritten Rules for Barcelona

1

Guard your belongings — always

Barcelona is one of the pickpocket capitals of Europe. La Rambla and the Gothic Quarter are the hotspots. Bag in front, phone in pocket. This isn't paranoia — it's the golden rule.

2

Never eat on La Rambla

Frozen paellas and overpriced drinks. Two streets back into any neighbourhood and the food transforms completely. Always walk away from the main boulevard to eat.

3

Book Gaudí tickets weeks ahead

Sagrada Família and Park Güell sell out constantly. No ticket = turned away at the door. Buy online, pick your time slot. No exceptions.
