Matera

A nine-thousand-year cave city: a story of stone, bread and rebirth

Overview

The Vibe of Matera

Arriving in Matera confuses time itself: the cave houses (sassi) carved into the ravine tell of a city inhabited without pause for nine thousand years. Emptied in the 1950s as 'the shame of Italy', the caves are today UNESCO-listed and reborn with cave hotels, stone lanes and terraces over the canyon. Mel Gibson shot 'The Passion' here; you'll understand at first glance — these stones are old enough to play Jerusalem.

💬 Vicinato
🗣️ /vee-chee-NAH-to/

The neighbourhood order built around a shared courtyard in the Sassi: shared well, shared oven, shared life. That solidarity was Matera's secret of survival; it lives on in the corner conversations today.

Insider Advice

Unwritten Rules for Matera

#1
Stay a night — Matera begins at dusk When the day-trippers leave, the stones of the Sassi fill with light and the city becomes another place. One night in a cave hotel is the difference between visiting Matera and living it.
#2
Don't drive into the Sassi The historic core is a ZTL (restricted traffic zone) and most lanes are stairs anyway. Leave the car in the upper-town car parks; use your hotel's luggage service, or come with a light bag.
#3
Solid shoes, a full water bottle Matera is an up-and-down city: cobbles, steps and summer sun. Heels and flip-flops don't walk here; double the water if you're descending into the canyon.
Off the Beaten Path

Hidden Gems in Matera

💎 HIDDEN GEM
Palombaro Lungo
A giant cistern of five thousand cubic metres under the main square: 'the cathedral of water'. In these underground vaults you grasp that the Sassi could never have lived without it; visited on a short tour.
💎 HIDDEN GEM
Cripta del Peccato Originale
Eighth-century frescoes in a rock church outside town: 'the Sistine Chapel of the rupestrian churches'. Visits by reservation only — worth every effort.
💎 HIDDEN GEM
Madonna de Idris
The church fused to the rock above Caveoso: a frescoed rock chapel within, one of the canyon's best views without. Climb near sunset.
💎 HIDDEN GEM
Murgia Timone Belvedere
The viewpoint on the far slope, reached over a suspension bridge: the Sassi head-on, like a film still. 'The Passion' was shot here; the sunset is watched here.
💎 HIDDEN GEM
MUSMA
A contemporary sculpture museum installed in a palazzo's cave floors: modern forms in stone hollows. The most elegant version of Matera's 'cave + art' equation.
💎 HIDDEN GEM
Casa Noha
FAI's short-film museum: Matera's journey from shame to pride in 25 minutes. See it BEFORE touring the Sassi — you'll look at the stones with new eyes.
Food & Flavors

What to Taste

Lucanian cooking is poverty's inventive genius: the bread sacred, the pepper crisp, the pasta handmade.

🍴 MUST TASTE
Pane di Matera
The legendary horn-shaped loaf with thick crust and yellow crumb (IGP protected): baked in wood ovens, fresh for a week. The city's scent drifts from these bakeries.
🍴 MUST TASTE
Peperoni cruschi
Crisp-fried dried peppers: a meze on their own, a crown on eggs and pasta. Buying a packet for the suitcase is entirely legitimate.
🍴 MUST TASTE
Cavatelli and orecchiette — with mollica
Handmade pasta with garlicky fried breadcrumbs (mollica) and cruschi: Matera's 'poor but royal' plate.
🍴 MUST TASTE
Crapiata
The vicinato soup of wheat, legumes and potato simmered low and slow: the neighbours' harvest celebration dish. It even has its own festival in early August.
🍴 MUST TASTE
Cialledda
Stale Matera bread resurrected with tomato, cucumber and olive oil: the cold 'poor salad' of summer, a warm version in winter. Respect for bread, turned into a plate.
🍴 MUST TASTE
Aglianico del Vulture
The powerful red of the volcanic Vulture slopes: 'the Barolo of the south'. Found by the glass in cave enotecas; its pairing with cruschi and cheese is a masterclass.
Watch Out

Tourist Traps to Avoid

❌
The satnav's offer to 'take you to the hotel door' ends in a camera-issued ZTL fine; reversing out of the narrow lanes is its own drama.
✅ Better Alternative: Leave the car in an upper-town car park and walk, or request the hotel pickup; confirm the ZTL hours with your hotel.
❌
Stepping off a coach, shooting the belvedere and leaving is not seeing Matera; the city's soul lives in evening light and inside the caves.
✅ Better Alternative: Stay a night: sunset, a night walk in the stone lanes and the morning bakery smell — that is Matera's real ticket.
❌
Some 'guides' who approach at the gates are unlicensed; you pay well for a memorised loop.
✅ Better Alternative: For a guided visit, book a licensed guide or the Casa Noha + rock churches combination in advance.
❌
Some panoramic terraces sell ready-made plates as a 'menu tipico'; the view is lovely, the cavatelli tired.
✅ Better Alternative: Choose the trattoria that talks about handmade pasta and the day's dish; among cave restaurants, the small one is usually the good one.
