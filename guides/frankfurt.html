Overview & Atmosphere

The True Vibe of Frankfurt

Financial capital of the continent and home to the European Central Bank, Frankfurt hides a storybook soul behind its gleaming skyscrapers: the timber-framed guildhalls of Römerberg, the birthplace of German democracy at Paulskirche, and the riverside museum promenade of Museumsufer. Across the Main River, the cobblestone alleys of Alt-Sachsenhausen invite you into candlelit cider taverns serving crisp Ebbelwoi from grey stoneware jugs.

🗣️ Local Concept: Ebbelwoi (eb-bel-voy)

Frankfurt's centuries-old tart, fermented apple wine, traditionally poured from grey ceramic 'Bembel' jugs into diamond-patterned 'Geripptes' glasses.

3 Unwritten Rules for Frankfurt

1

Drink Ebbelwoi in an authentic Sachsenhausen tavern

Never drink mass-produced cider; sit at communal pine tables in Alt-Sachsenhausen (like Wagner or Dauth-Schneider) and order a 'Schoppen' of pure apple wine.

2

Feel history at Römerberg and Paulskirche

Walk among the faithfully reconstructed half-timbered merchant houses and visit Paulskirche, cradle of Germany's first freely elected parliament in 1848.

3

Stroll along the Museumsufer riverbank

Cross the historic Eiserner Steg footbridge and stroll along the south bank of the Main, flanked by world-class art museums and skyline vistas.

Getting Around

Frankfurt Public Transit & Airport Transfers

RMV's ultra-reliable S-Bahn, U-Bahn, and tram network makes traveling across Frankfurt fast and effortless.

🚆

Airport S-Bahn (S8 & S9)

Departs every 15 minutes from Terminal 1 directly to Frankfurt Central Station and Hauptwache.

🚇

U-Bahn & Trams

Nine underground lines and extensive trams connect business districts, Sachsenhausen, and Museumsufer.

🚶

Walking the Altstadt

The newly rebuilt New Old Town (Neue Altstadt), Cathedral, and Main riverbanks are all under 10 minutes on foot.

🚲

Cycling the Main River

Dedicated flat cycling paths line both banks of the Main, offering uninterrupted city and park riding.

🎫

Frankfurt Card

Free transit throughout Frankfurt (including the airport) plus up to 50% discount on museums and Main river cruises.

💡 Frankfurt Card Savings

Visiting the Städel Museum and taking the airport train already saves you money with the 1-day card.

From the historic charm of Römer to the rustic vibe of Sachsenhausen and the modern high-rises of Westend.

Historic & Central

Seasons & Weather

Best Time to Visit Frankfurt

From spring apple blossom festivals to summer riverside terraces and winter glühwein markets.

Spring

Apr – Jun
Green Sauce & Blossom Season

May brings the beloved Grüne Soße Festival on Roßmarkt celebrating the city's signature seven-herb sauce.

Summer

Jul – Aug
Museumsuferfest & River Vibe

Over 2 million visitors gather along the Main for three days of world music, open-air art, and synchronized fireworks.

Autumn

Sep – Oct
Frankfurt Book Fair & Fresh Cider

The world's largest book fair (Frankfurter Buchmesse) electrifies the city alongside freshly pressed 'Süßer' cloudy cider.

Winter

Nov – Dec
Römerberg Christmas Market

Centuries of holiday tradition centered beneath a giant illuminated spruce in front of the historic Römer town hall.

Culinary Culture

Authentic Local Dining & Food Secrets in Frankfurt

Fresh herbal Grüne Soße, tangy Handkäs mit Musik, and delicate Bethmännchen marzipan.

🌿

Frankfurter Grüne Soße

Cold, creamy herb sauce made strictly from seven fresh local herbs (borage, chervil, cress, parsley, burnet, sorrel, chives), served with boiled eggs and potatoes.

⭐ City Signature
🌭

Frankfurter Würstchen

Pure pork smoked sausages in natural sheep casing, gently heated in hot water and served with sharp mustard and crusty rolls.

⭐ Historic Sausage
🧀

Handkäs mit Musik

Translucent sour-milk cheese topped with diced raw onions and caraway; spread onto dense rye sourdough with thick farm butter.

⭐ Tavern Classic
🥩

Frankfurter Rippchen mit Kraut

Cured and gently cooked pork ribs served steaming on a mountain of wine sauerkraut with creamy mashed potatoes.

⭐ Hearty Main
🍬

Bethmännchen

Traditional marzipan confectionery flavored with rosewater and adorned with three halved almonds representing the Bethmann brothers.

⭐ Marzipan Classic
🎂

Frankfurter Kranz

Crown-shaped sponge cake layered with rich buttercream and red berry jam, coated in crunchy caramelized hazelnut brittle (Krokant).

⭐ Crown Cake
🍷 Kleinmarkthalle Butcher

Join the queue at Metzgerei Schreiber inside Kleinmarkthalle for hot, fresh Fleischwurst with mustard on butcher paper.

Off The Beaten Path

Handpicked Hidden Gems in Frankfurt

Quiet river wine terraces, medieval castle suburbs, and secluded sculpture gardens.

✨ Historic Suburb

Höchst Historic Old Town

Just 10 minutes west by S-Bahn, Höchst feels like a centuries-old village with timbered houses, a moated castle, and porcelain manufactory.

✨ Secret Garden

Liebieghaus Sculpture Garden Cafe

A tranquil 19th-century villa garden on Museumsufer featuring antique marble sculptures and a hidden terrace cafe under sycamores.

✨ Water Castle

Holzhausen Park & Baroque Moat

A serene North End park centered on a miniature baroque moated castle where classical concerts echo across reflecting ponds.

✨ Cathedral Vista

Kaiserdom Imperial Cloister & Tower

Climb 328 stone steps up the red sandstone tower of St. Bartholomew's Cathedral for an extraordinary direct look down at Römerberg.

✨ Rooftop Vibe

Kleinmarkthalle Upper Terrace Wine Bar

Head up to the market hall's mezzanine terrace on Saturday afternoon to sip local Riesling alongside lively Frankfurt residents.

✨ Panoramic Hill

Lohrberg Orchard Hill Viewpoint

Frankfurt's only remaining vineyard hill in Seckbach; enjoy apple wine and rustic snacks with a sweeping vista of the entire skyscraper skyline.

✨ Lohrberg Sunset

Take bus 43 to Lohrberg on a clear late afternoon for the most romantic sunset picnic view over Frankfurt.

Scam Prevention

Tourist Traps to Avoid & Smarter Alternatives

How to experience Frankfurt like an insider without falling into tourist traps.

🍏 TRAP

Asking for sweet lemonade in Ebbelwoi

Purists drink apple wine pure ('Pur') or with sparkling mineral water ('Sauergespritzer'). Adding sweet lemonade ('Süßgespritzer') will draw stern looks in traditional taverns.

💡 What to Do Instead: Order 'Einen Schoppen Sauer' for a refreshing spritzer.
🍽️ TRAP

Dining only on Römerberg square

Terraces directly facing Römerberg charge tourist premiums; walk 5 minutes across the bridge into Sachsenhausen for authentic family-run taverns.

💡 What to Do Instead: Cross Eiserner Steg into Schweizer Straße.
🚉 TRAP

Hanging around the station forecourt at night

While safe during daylight, the streets immediately facing the central station can feel rough after dark.

💡 What to Do Instead: Use the underground U-Bahn or stick to Kaiserstraße's lit avenues.
🪙 TRAP

Using credit cards in old cider cellars

Many historic cider taverns in Sachsenhausen remain strictly cash-only (Nur Barzahlung).

💡 What to Do Instead: Keep €20–€40 cash in your wallet before entering.
🛡️ Communal Tables

In Sachsenhausen apple wine taverns, strangers always sit together on long benches. Simply ask 'Ist hier frei?' and make new friends!

Local Slang

Useful Everyday Phrases in Frankfurt

Charming expressions from the Frankfurt and Rhine-Main dialect.

🗣️ Gude!
The ultimate friendly Frankfurt greeting for any time of day.
🗣️ Bembel
The traditional grey salt-glazed ceramic jug used to serve apple wine.
🗣️ Geripptes
The classic faceted cider glass that keeps your hand from slipping on grease.
🗣️ Ei gude, wie?
'Hey there, how are you doing?'—local informal conversation starter.
🗣️ Dankeschön
Warm and appreciative 'Thank you very much'.
