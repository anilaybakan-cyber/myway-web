Zurich

The lake city hidden behind the banks: river swims, Dada and chocolate

Overview

The Vibe of Zurich

Zurich's fame was written by its banks, but the city's secret lies elsewhere: on summer lunchtimes the suited jump into the Limmat, the old town's steeples hide Chagall windows, and the Dada movement was born on a bar stage here. The industrial district's viaduct arches have turned into design shops, the trams run like clockwork, and the lake poses with its mountain skyline every evening. Zurich looks serious — but keep your swimsuit in your bag: this city knows better than anyone how to strip off and jump in.

🗣️ Local Concept: Badi (bah-dee)

The heart of Zurich's open-air swimming culture: the wooden bathing decks along lake and river. Swimming in the lunch break is a civic tradition — and some badis become bars at night, glasses raised with feet in the water.

Insider Advice

3 Unwritten Rules for Zurich

1

Take the river swim seriously — and join it

The Limmat and the lake are clean enough to swim, and the locals do it daily: use the marked badi areas, learn the exit ladders, and float your belongings in a waterproof bag (a Wickelfisch). Whoever doesn't swim has seen half of Zurich.

2

Respect the quiet hours

Swiss order runs through daily life: night and Sunday quiet are taken seriously, and tram phone calls happen at half volume. The rules aren't coldness — they're everyone's comfort; adapt and relax.

3

Manage the prices with strategy

Zurich is among the world's most expensive cities: lunch menus, bakeries, supermarket terraces and the free lake-and-river life strike the balance. The fountain water is drinkable everywhere — 1,200 fountains are the city's free bar.
