Kotor

A fjord-like bay, medieval walls and the city's true owners: the cats

Overview

The Vibe of Kotor

Kotor stands where mountains suit the sea best: black cliffs plunge straight into the water, the bay curls like a fjord, and the medieval town sits at the bottom of this stage set like a seal pressed in wax. Venetian lions wait at its gates, cats in its squares, walls on the ridge above. The cruise crowds come at noon and leave by evening; you stay — the true sound of the stone lanes is heard at night, between the bells and the gulls.

🗣️ Local Concept: Boka (bo-ka)

The local name of the Bay of Kotor: 'Boka Kotorska'. The bay's people are called Bokelji, and it is an identity — it carries a seafaring tradition, a 12-century-old naval brotherhood and a heavy, particular pride.

Insider Advice

3 Unwritten Rules for Kotor

1

Live by the cruise ship schedule

The ships dock in the morning, and between 11 and 4 the Old Town overflows. Set your wall climb and square time for 8 am or late afternoon; save midday for the bay villages.

2

Respect the cats — they're the town's totem

Cats are Kotor's symbol: this crew, descended from sailors' ships, has protected the town from rats and snakes for centuries. Feeding is allowed, chasing is shameful; they even have a museum.

3

For the walls, pay the ticket or know the path

Official entry to the San Giovanni fortress is ticketed; but the Ladder of Kotor trail, starting outside town, connects to the walls through a window. On either route, water and morning cool are essential.
