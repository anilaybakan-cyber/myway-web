Rhodes

Knights' walls, three hundred days of sun, and an island where two seas meet

✍️ MyWay Editorial Team • 🛡️ Verified Local Guide
Overview & Atmosphere

The True Vibe of Rhodes

As the ferry nears Mandraki harbour, the walls salute you from the Middle Ages: Rhodes is a Mediterranean island holding one of Europe's best-preserved medieval cities. Inside the walls, knights' lanes; outside, palm-lined Italian boulevards; and to the south stretches an island of wind and sea. An hour from the Turkish coast lies another world — though don't be surprised when the tea and the conversation feel familiar.

🗣️ Local Concept: Filoxenia (fee-lo-kse-NEE-ah)

Greek for 'love of the stranger': hospitality's ancient name. The free dessert at the table, the shopkeeper walking you to your turn, the pension owner saying 'sit, have a coffee' — all daily forms of filoxenia.

3 Unwritten Rules for Rhodes

1

Live the Old Town at morning or evening

By midday the walled city surrenders to cruise crowds. Before 9 am and after 6 pm the lanes belong to locals and to you; the stone glows differently in evening light.

2

Don't plan the island from one point

Rhodes is big: Lindos is 55 km away, Prasonisi 90 km south. Rent a car or plan around the bus timetable; the 'everything is close' illusion ends the day on the road.

3

Sort the ferry paperwork early

On the Marmaris and Fethiye lines, book ahead and arrive early for passport control; confirm your return sailing on day one. The summer wind (meltemi) sometimes rewrites the schedule.

Getting Around

Rhodes Public Transit & Airport Transfers

At the northern tip everything is close; for the rest of the island you need wheels or patience.

⛴️

Ferries — Marmaris and the islands

About an hour from Marmaris by catamaran, daily in summer. Big ferries from Piraeus, and day boats to the neighbouring islands (Symi, Halki) leave from Mandraki.

🚌

KTEL buses — two coasts

The east line (toward Lindos) and the west line leave from separate terminals; Lindos runs frequently. Cheap and air-conditioned — but always check the last return.

🚗

Rental car — the key to the south

The only real option for Prasonisi, Monolithos and the west coast. Roads are good, traffic calm; petrol stations thin out in the south. Book ahead in summer.

🛵

Scooters and ATVs

Practical around town; but the meltemi blows seriously on the west side. Car for the long hauls, scooter for short scouting — that's the right formula.

🚕

Taxis

Short city rides are reasonable; for long routes like Lindos, fix the price before boarding. The airport-city fare list hangs at the rank.

💡 Symi tip

Give one day to Symi: 50 minutes by morning boat from Mandraki, and a pastel neoclassical amphitheatre of a harbour awaits. That's where the Symi shrimp is eaten — and the return boat is never missed.

Neighborhood Guide

Where to Stay in Rhodes: Best Areas

Inside the walls amid history, in a beach town, or in the empty south — on Rhodes all three are possible.

Old Town (inside the walls)

Most atmospheric

Stone-mansion guesthouses inside the medieval lanes: the city is yours before the crowds arrive, and at night the lantern-lit streets are a film set. Car access is limited — luggage gets walked — but it's worth it.

📍 Medieval fabric • Night magic • Luggage on foot

New Town / Mandraki

Most practical

Harbour, market, cafés and the town beach within walking distance; the ideal base if you arrive by ferry. The Italian-era architecture and the evening promenade are a surprise pleasure.

📍 Near the ferry • Town beach • Promenade evenings

Lindos

Most postcard

A whitewashed village under an acropolis, with two bays: Rhodes's most famous view. Very crowded on summer days, but the village calms at dusk; for honeymooners and view-chasers.

📍 Acropolis views • St Paul's Bay • Quiet evenings

Ialysos / Ixia — the windward side

Surfer friendly

The west coast's breezy beaches are among Europe's favourite windsurf and kite spots; hotels are large, sunsets long. For those who like their sea with waves.

📍 Windsurfing • Sunsets • Large resorts

The south — toward Gennadi/Monolithos

Calmest

Where the crowds end: empty beaches, village tavernas and a starry sky. A car is essential; the reward is the island at its purest.

📍 Empty beaches • Village tavernas • Car essential
Seasons & Weather

Best Time to Visit Rhodes

Rhodes is the island of the sun; the question is never 'will it shine' but 'how many others will be there'.

Spring

Apr – Jun
Green Rhodes

The island at its greenest, the Valley of the Butterflies still calm, the Old Town at strolling temperature. In June the sea warms while the crowds stay below July levels.

Summer

Jul – Aug
Full Season

The eastern bays lie warm and still, the west side cools in the meltemi; nightlife and boat trips peak. On cruise days the Old Town overflows at noon — the morning/evening rule saves lives.

Autumn

Sep – Nov
The Golden Sea

September and October are the island's crown: the sea like a bath, the crowds receded, the prices down. Even in November, sunny lunches happen by the water.

Winter

Dec – Mar
The Locals' Island

Tourist facilities partly close but the city keeps living; you walk the Old Town nearly alone. Walking weather between rain breaks — and the year's most honest prices.

Culinary Culture

Authentic Local Dining & Food Secrets in Rhodes

The Rhodes kitchen is island Greece at its heartiest: fried meze, fresh fish and wine down from the mountain villages.

🧆

Pitaroudia — chickpea fritters

Rhodes's signature meze: chickpeas kneaded with tomato and mint, then fried; crisp outside, soft within. One of ouzo's best friends.

⭐ Ouzeri classic · while hot
🐟

The day's fish — grilled

Bream, red mullet or whatever the meltemi brought: char-grilled, with lemon, by the water. Choosing your fish from the display by weight is normal here.

⭐ Harbour tavernas · pick by weight
🦐

Symi shrimp

The neighbouring island's tiny shrimp are fried whole and eaten shell and all: crisp, salty, addictive. Found in Rhodes tavernas too — ask for today's.

⭐ Eat with the shell · Symi style
🫓

Oven lamb of the mountain villages

The plate of the highlands (toward Embonas): lamb or goat, hours in the oven, island pasta alongside. The feast of Sunday afternoons.

⭐ Mountain tavernas · Sunday lunch
🍷

The wines of Embonas

Embonas, the vineyard village under Mount Attavyros, is the island's wine capital: Athiri whites and Amorgiano reds. Village winery tastings are warm and generous.

⭐ Village tasting · ask for Athiri
🍯

Melekouni and talagoutes

The sesame-honey wedding sweet melekouni and honey-drizzled village pancakes talagoutes: the island's sweet memory. Fresh at markets and village festivals.

⭐ Market stalls · gifts too
🍷 The taverna rule

In the Old Town, avoid the 'doorway hawker + multilingual photo menu' pairing; one lane back, the place chalking its menu feeds you twice as well for the same money.

Off The Beaten Path

Handpicked Hidden Gems in Rhodes

Rhodes's celebrities live on postcards; these six are the reward of whoever takes the car keys.

✨ A castle on the cliff's edge

Monolithos Castle

A knights' castle set atop a 236-metre rock on the west coast: a few walls, a chapel and an endless sea. At sunset, the island's most dramatic stage.

✨ Where two seas meet

Prasonisi

At the island's southern tip, the two sides of a sand spit: one sea calm, the other the wind's. Mecca for kitesurfers; even watching is a show.

✨ An oasis in plane-tree shade

Seven Springs (Epta Piges)

Streams, peacocks and a dark water tunnel inside a pine forest: walking the tunnel out to the lake is the island's classic dare.

✨ A ruin with a view

Kritinia Castle

A ruined knights' castle looking over the west coast and Halki island: free entry, no crowds, a free sunset. A village taverna nearby is the bonus.

✨ A bay from the movies

Anthony Quinn Bay

The turquoise cove named after 'The Guns of Navarone': a snorkelling paradise among the rocks. Go early — the boats arrive at noon.

✨ Italian traces on a hilltop

Filerimos and the Golgotha way

Above ancient Ialysos: a monastery, a cypress-lined 'Golgotha' walk and a giant cross; peacocks accompany you, and both coasts show below.

✨ Southern tip

On your day south, take lunch at a village taverna in Gennadi or Lachania; the menu is short, the fish fresh, the bill half.

Scam Prevention

Tourist Traps to Avoid & Smarter Alternatives

Rhodes is an honest island; the traps are squeezed along the cruise route. Know these four — the rest is filoxenia.

🍽️ TRAP

The hawkered tourist menu inside the walls

Around Socratous Street, the places calling you in with photo menus sell frozen moussaka at castle prices.

💡 What to Do Instead: Go a lane or two deeper, or step outside the walls: at chalk-menu ouzeris the same money buys the real thing.
🏖️ TRAP

Mistaking Faliraki for the whole island

The party beach's bars and jet-ski noise are not Rhodes's shop window; ending the island there leaves it half-seen.

💡 What to Do Instead: Give a day to the south: Gennadi's emptiness, Prasonisi's wind, Monolithos's cliff — the island's true face is there.
🛵 TRAP

Renting an uninsured ATV

The cheap-looking quads are dangerous on windy roads and ruinous in a scrape; licence and insurance checks get skipped.

💡 What to Do Instead: Rent a car or a fully insured scooter; never sign without reading the damage excess.
⛵ TRAP

Haggling an 'all-inclusive boat tour' at the harbour

Some tours charge on board for what was 'included', and on a packed deck the cove loses its charm.

💡 What to Do Instead: Ask in writing what's included (food, drinks, stops); the scheduled boats to Symi or Halki are usually the better deal.
🛡️ The golden rule

When a cruise ship is in port, leave the Old Town for the south or the beach; when it sails at dusk, the walls are yours again.

Local Slang

Useful Everyday Phrases in Rhodes

Three words of Greek open the doors; arriving from the neighbouring shore guarantees a smile on top.

🗣️ Yassas
Hello / goodbye — the formal, safe greeting
🗣️ Efharistó
Thank you — looks hard, lands by the second try
🗣️ Parakaló
Please / you're welcome — Greek's joker card
🗣️ Yamas!
Cheers! — ouzo glasses rise to this
🗣️ Kalimera
Good morning — the word that lights up faces at the morning market
