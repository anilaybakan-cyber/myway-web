Rhodes

Knights' walls, three hundred days of sun, and an island where two seas meet

Overview

The Vibe of Rhodes

As the ferry nears Mandraki harbour, the walls salute you from the Middle Ages: Rhodes is a Mediterranean island holding one of Europe's best-preserved medieval cities. Inside the walls, knights' lanes; outside, palm-lined Italian boulevards; and to the south stretches an island of wind and sea. An hour from the Turkish coast lies another world — though don't be surprised when the tea and the conversation feel familiar.

💬 Filoxenia
🗣️ /fee-lo-kse-NEE-ah/

Greek for 'love of the stranger': hospitality's ancient name. The free dessert at the table, the shopkeeper walking you to your turn, the pension owner saying 'sit, have a coffee' — all daily forms of filoxenia.

Insider Advice

Unwritten Rules for Rhodes

#1
Live the Old Town at morning or evening By midday the walled city surrenders to cruise crowds. Before 9 am and after 6 pm the lanes belong to locals and to you; the stone glows differently in evening light.
#2
Don't plan the island from one point Rhodes is big: Lindos is 55 km away, Prasonisi 90 km south. Rent a car or plan around the bus timetable; the 'everything is close' illusion ends the day on the road.
#3
Sort the ferry paperwork early On the Marmaris and Fethiye lines, book ahead and arrive early for passport control; confirm your return sailing on day one. The summer wind (meltemi) sometimes rewrites the schedule.
Off the Beaten Path

Hidden Gems in Rhodes

💎 HIDDEN GEM
Monolithos Castle
A knights' castle set atop a 236-metre rock on the west coast: a few walls, a chapel and an endless sea. At sunset, the island's most dramatic stage.
💎 HIDDEN GEM
Prasonisi
At the island's southern tip, the two sides of a sand spit: one sea calm, the other the wind's. Mecca for kitesurfers; even watching is a show.
💎 HIDDEN GEM
Seven Springs (Epta Piges)
Streams, peacocks and a dark water tunnel inside a pine forest: walking the tunnel out to the lake is the island's classic dare.
💎 HIDDEN GEM
Kritinia Castle
A ruined knights' castle looking over the west coast and Halki island: free entry, no crowds, a free sunset. A village taverna nearby is the bonus.
💎 HIDDEN GEM
Anthony Quinn Bay
The turquoise cove named after 'The Guns of Navarone': a snorkelling paradise among the rocks. Go early — the boats arrive at noon.
💎 HIDDEN GEM
Filerimos and the Golgotha way
Above ancient Ialysos: a monastery, a cypress-lined 'Golgotha' walk and a giant cross; peacocks accompany you, and both coasts show below.
Food & Flavors

What to Taste

The Rhodes kitchen is island Greece at its heartiest: fried meze, fresh fish and wine down from the mountain villages.

🍴 MUST TASTE
Pitaroudia — chickpea fritters
Rhodes's signature meze: chickpeas kneaded with tomato and mint, then fried; crisp outside, soft within. One of ouzo's best friends.
🍴 MUST TASTE
The day's fish — grilled
Bream, red mullet or whatever the meltemi brought: char-grilled, with lemon, by the water. Choosing your fish from the display by weight is normal here.
🍴 MUST TASTE
Symi shrimp
The neighbouring island's tiny shrimp are fried whole and eaten shell and all: crisp, salty, addictive. Found in Rhodes tavernas too — ask for today's.
🍴 MUST TASTE
Oven lamb of the mountain villages
The plate of the highlands (toward Embonas): lamb or goat, hours in the oven, island pasta alongside. The feast of Sunday afternoons.
🍴 MUST TASTE
The wines of Embonas
Embonas, the vineyard village under Mount Attavyros, is the island's wine capital: Athiri whites and Amorgiano reds. Village winery tastings are warm and generous.
🍴 MUST TASTE
Melekouni and talagoutes
The sesame-honey wedding sweet melekouni and honey-drizzled village pancakes talagoutes: the island's sweet memory. Fresh at markets and village festivals.
Watch Out

Tourist Traps to Avoid

❌
Around Socratous Street, the places calling you in with photo menus sell frozen moussaka at castle prices.
✅ Better Alternative: Go a lane or two deeper, or step outside the walls: at chalk-menu ouzeris the same money buys the real thing.
❌
The party beach's bars and jet-ski noise are not Rhodes's shop window; ending the island there leaves it half-seen.
✅ Better Alternative: Give a day to the south: Gennadi's emptiness, Prasonisi's wind, Monolithos's cliff — the island's true face is there.
❌
The cheap-looking quads are dangerous on windy roads and ruinous in a scrape; licence and insurance checks get skipped.
✅ Better Alternative: Rent a car or a fully insured scooter; never sign without reading the damage excess.
❌
Some tours charge on board for what was 'included', and on a packed deck the cove loses its charm.
✅ Better Alternative: Ask in writing what's included (food, drinks, stops); the scheduled boats to Symi or Halki are usually the better deal.
