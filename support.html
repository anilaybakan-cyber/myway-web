Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a route?

Open the app, select your city, and tap on "Create Route" or use the AI assistant to generate personalized routes based on your preferences.

How do I manage my subscription?

You can manage or cancel your subscription through your device's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → MyWay.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app, go to the subscription screen, and tap "Restore Purchases" at the bottom.

Is my data secure?

Yes! We take your privacy seriously. Please review our Privacy Policy for details on how we protect your information.

Which cities are available?

We currently support 30+ popular travel destinations including Paris, Rome, Barcelona, Tokyo, New York, and many more. New cities are added regularly!

Does the app work offline?

Yes! MyWay offers full offline support for maps and guides. Download your city data before traveling and explore without worrying about roaming fees.
