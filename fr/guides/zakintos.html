Aperçu & Atmosphère

La Véritable Âme de Zakynthos

Célèbre dans le monde entier pour sa spectaculaire plage du Naufrage cernée de falaises calcaires étincelantes, Zante (Zakynthos) offre des grottes marines aux reflets féeriques et des sanctuaires protégés pour les tortues.

🗣️ Concept Local: Ladotyri (lah-doh-tee-ree)

Zakynthos's traditional hard, sharp, salty sheep-and-goat milk cheese, aged for months preserved in pure local extra-virgin olive oil in clay vessels.

3 Règles Non Écrites pour Zakynthos

1

Visit the Navagio Shipwreck clifftop viewpoint in the morning

Arrive at the clifftop metal viewing platform above Navagio before 10:00 AM for clear, calm morning sunlight directly illuminating the electric blue waters.

2

Respect the Caretta-caretta turtle nesting beaches

Laganas Bay and Gerakas Beach are protected national marine parks; never touch turtles while swimming, keep distance, and obey nighttime beach curfews.

3

Watch the sunset from the cliffs of Keri or Kambi

Drive to the western lighthouse of Keri or the cross at Kambi to watch the sun dip directly into the deep Ionian Sea behind the dramatic Mizithres sea stacks.

Transports en Commun & Transferts Aéroport à Zakynthos

Hiring a car, scooter, or quad bike is the best way to explore cliffs, combined with local boat tours.

Car ~€30 - €50/day · Scooter ~€20/day

The island is 40 km long; a car or 125cc scooter gives you freedom to reach remote western cliffs and beaches.

Boat trip ~€20 - €25

Boats depart continuously from Porto Vromi or Agios Nikolaos port to cruise right inside the Blue Caves.

Turtle cruise ~€15 - €20

Electric or slow-propeller boats departing Agios Sostis to view Caretta-caretta turtles peacefully in Laganas Bay.

Single ticket (€1.80 - €3.50)

Hors des Sentiers Battus

Pépites Cachées Sélectionnées à Zakynthos

Limestone sea fjords, wooden footbridges to private islets, and colossal sea stacks.

Porto Limnionas Sea Fjord

A secluded, narrow turquoise sea canyon carved into wild limestone cliffs, featuring sunbathing flat rocks and sub-aquatic sea caves.

Mizithres Sea Stacks Lookout

Two colossal pointed chalk sea stacks rising from electric blue waters off Keri, recently joined by a breathtaking white pebble beach formed by an earthquake.

Cameo Island & Wooden Rope Bridge

A tiny pine-covered rock islet in Agios Sostis linked to the mainland by a picturesque swinging wooden footbridge over crystal water.

Anafonitria 14th-Century Monastery

A peaceful Byzantine fortress monastery in the pine mountains where island patron Saint Dionysios lived as an abbot in the 16th century.

Bochali Hill & Venetian Castle

The pine-shaded hilltop terrace above Zakynthos Town, offering stunning views over the entire crescent harbor, port, and blue Ionian Sea.

Plakaki Bay Rocky Cliff Path

A thrilling, narrow staircase carved into sheer sea cliffs near Agalas village leading to an extraordinary secluded flat-rock swimming cove.
