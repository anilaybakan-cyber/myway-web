Descripción & Ambiente

El Verdadero Ambiente de Corfu

Cubierta de millones de olivos y rodeada por el mar Jónico, Corfú combina la arquitectura señorial veneciana de su casco histórico protegido con playas idílicas y recoletas bahías de ensueño.

🗣️ Concepto Local: Kantounia (kahn-too-nyah)

The narrow, winding, cobblestone Venetian alleyways of Corfu Old Town, barely wide enough for two people, shaded by multi-story pastel mansions.

3 Reglas No Escritas para Corfu

1

Get lost in the Campiello quarter without a map

Wander through the oldest Venetian residential district of the Old Town, discovering tiny cobblestone squares, hidden chapels, and wrought-iron balconies.

2

Arrive at Paleokastritsa Monastery in the morning

Perched on a dramatic clifftop above twin turquoise bays, visit the 13th-century monastery early before taking a small boat into the sea caves.

3

Sip an iced frappe beneath the Liston arcades

Modeled after Paris's Rue de Rivoli by French architects, sit under the lanterned arches facing the green Spianada cricket pitch for classic people-watching.

Fuera de lo Común

Joyas Ocultas Seleccionadas en Corfu

Twin sea coves, Byzantine eagles' nests, and sculpted sandstone fjords.

Vlacherna Monastery & Mouse Island

A 17th-century whitewashed nunnery connected to Kanoni by a narrow pedestrian sea causeway, framed by green cypress islets.

Porto Timoni Twin Beach

A dramatic isthmus separating two simultaneous turquoise bays near Afionas, reached via a rewarding 20-minute scenic cliff hike.

Achilleion Palace of Empress Sisi

Neoclassical summer palace built in 1890 for Empress Elisabeth of Austria, adorned with dramatic marble statues of Dying Achilles.

Angelokastro (Castle of Angels)

A sheer 300-meter clifftop Byzantine fortress perched precariously over the Ionian Sea, offering panoramic views of the entire island.

Cape Drastis Sandstone Cliffs

Spectacular weathered white clay cliffs and dramatic finger-like sea stacks jutting into turquoise waters on the northwest tip.

Gouvia Venetian Naval Shipyard

Three soaring 18th-century stone archways surviving from the colossal Venetian naval drydock and arsenal on Gouvia Bay.

Itinerario Inteligente

Día 1: Recorrido a Pie Paso a Paso por Corfu

🗺️ Mapa Interactivo de Ruta del Día 1 MAPA EN VIVO
7 Paradas
09:00

#1 Old Venetian Fortress & Lighthouse

Cross the moat bridge into the 15th-century sea citadel and climb to the lighthouse for views of the Old Town.

10:30

#2 Liston Arcades & Spianada Square

Stroll beneath the Napoleonic lanterns and sip a cold iced frappe or fresh ginger beer (Tsitsibira).

11:30

#3 Campiello Quarter & St. Spyridon Church

Wander through the narrow laundry-strung alleys and pay respects to the island's patron saint.

13:00

#4 Venetian Lunch at Rex Restaurant

Taste authentic beef Sofrito in garlic-wine sauce or rooster Pastitsada on a pedestrian lane.

15:00

#5 Drive to Paleokastritsa Monastery

Visit the 13th-century clifftop monastery and take in breathtaking vistas over twin emerald bays.

16:30

#6 Sea Cave Boat Tour & Swimming

Board a small wooden boat into the luminous Blue Eye cave, then swim in the crystal-clear waters.

19:00

#7 Sunset Cocktail at Bella Vista

Head up to the village of Lakones (the 'Balcony of the Ionian') to watch the sunset over Paleokastritsa's bays.
