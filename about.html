About MyWay

Empowering travelers worldwide to discover authentic culture, explore step-by-step walking routes, and experience cities like true locals.

🧭 Our Mission & Vision

Travel is not about ticking off crowded tourist checkpoints; it is about immersing yourself in the rhythm of a city, finding hidden architectural masterpieces, tasting local cuisine in quiet alleys, and creating unforgettable memories.

MyWay was created to solve a universal travel headache: spending dozens of hours browsing scattered blogs, generic TripAdvisor top-10 lists, and static PDF maps. We built an intelligent, beautifully designed platform that combines curated local walking routes, neighborhood secrets, and real-time offline exploration tools in one unified ecosystem.

✨ Editorial Standards & Quality Policy

At MyWay, trust and authenticity are our top priorities. Every single recommendation in our web guides and mobile app is vetted against strict editorial standards:

• Genuine Local Perspective: We intentionally filter out overpriced tourist traps and low-value sponsored attractions. Our team emphasizes neighborhood bistros, historic artisan workshops, scenic vantage points, and cultural landmarks that true locals cherish.

• Continuous Updates via Cloud OTA: Travel information changes rapidly. Through our cloud over-the-air (OTA) content delivery pipeline, our destination guides, venue descriptions, opening tips, and curated walking paths are updated continuously without requiring manual app updates.

• Unbiased & Independent: None of the places featured in our curated city routes pay for inclusion. We believe travelers deserve honest, transparent advice to make the most of their precious time.
